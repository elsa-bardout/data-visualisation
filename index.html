Build a Sankey chart

Paste a spreadsheet, check its columns, then customise and download your chart.

Paste into any cell. Tab moves between cells; Enter moves down. Backspace edits text. Use Delete with Shift selected cells to clear a range. Undo restores edits and resets.

Automatic mode supports wide tables and multiple series. Explicit mapping uses the selected columns and keeps your source table intact.

Use Save project to keep your data and chart settings.
Chart data is processed in your browser. Maps send location fields to Postcodes.io or Esri for geographic lookup; basemap providers receive tile requests. Avoid personal or sensitive location data.
