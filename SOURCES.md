# Sample sources and interpretation

The sample button cycles independently within each chart type. The curated samples use published figures or public locations, not fictional measures. Source URLs stay in the editable table. Retrieved 9 November 2026; these are dated snapshots, not live feeds.

- **Animal sleep:** [University of Washington, Eric Chudler](https://faculty.washington.edu/chudler/chasleep.html), a research-based compilation with references. Values are published average hours per day, not universal limits. Awake hours are calculated as 24 minus sleep. The flow example sums one day for each selected species; it does not represent a population of animals.
- **Food production:** [Eurostat, Fruit and vegetable production in 2022](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/en/web/products-eurostat-news/w/ddn-20240301-1). Harvest amounts are million tonnes. Other vegetables = 59.8 − 15.4 − 6.2 − 4.4 = 33.8. Country flows multiply the published crop total by the rounded published share, so are approximate derived quantities. The Europe map compares each country's share of the named crop, not shares of all vegetables; crop names and denominators appear in the table and tooltip.
- **Planets:** [NASA Planetary Fact Sheet](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/). Diameter is km; gravity m/s²; distance million km; orbital period days; solar day hours. The line example connects discrete planetary observations in distance order, not a time series. The surprising long Venus day means solar day, not rotation period.
- **London census:** ONS [Camden](https://www.ons.gov.uk/visualisations/censusareachanges/E09000007/), [Islington](https://www.ons.gov.uk/visualisations/censusareachanges/E09000019/), [Hackney](https://www.ons.gov.uk/visualisations/censusareachanges/E09000012/), [Westminster](https://www.ons.gov.uk/visualisations/censusareachanges/E09000033/). Population figures use the articles' rounded counts, with each row linked to its borough. Working-hours shares use the Islington comparison table; rounded shares need not add to exactly 100%.
- **Camden:** real ward names and the five neighbourhood groups supplied in the design reference; library postcodes from [Camden Council](https://www.camden.gov.uk/libraries-opening-times). This sample has no invented activity, household or population counts. Postcode pins indicate postcode locations, not surveyed building entrances.
- **British museums:** addresses/postcodes from the museums themselves: [British Lawnmower Museum](https://www.lawnmowerworld.co.uk/contactUs.php), [Eureka!](https://eureka.org.uk/contact/), [Natural History Museum](https://www.nhm.ac.uk/visit.html), [National Museum of Scotland](https://www.nms.ac.uk/scotland). Categories are editorial descriptions, not quantitative data.

## Country boundaries

`assets/countries.geojson` contains the 177 features from Natural Earth's 1:110m admin-0 countries dataset, with properties reduced to country name and ISO codes. Downloaded from the [Natural Earth repository](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson). [Natural Earth data are public domain](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/). These simplified boundaries suit continental maps; small countries and detailed coasts are not all represented. They are not legal boundary definitions. UK councils and wards continue to use the ONS/Esri boundary service.

## Supported input formats

- CSV, quoted multiline CSV, tab-separated and semicolon-separated text; UTF-8 BOM accepted. Upload is CSV/TSV/text, not binary Excel workbooks. Excel ranges can be pasted.
- Maps: UK wards/councils and codes, UK postcodes, latitude/longitude, full addresses, location names, countries/ISO codes. Case and punctuation are normalised for header matching. Full addresses use online geocoding with a confidence threshold; add town, postcode and country to reduce ambiguity. Unmatched rows are reported rather than silently substituted. Country coverage is limited to the bundled geography above.
- Alluvial: source/target/value rows, weighted wide pathways, stage-based tables and node/link ID tables. Period and metadata columns are excluded from wide pathways.
- Donut: category/amount columns, category/period/value rows, or periods in rows with categories in columns. Values must be positive; duplicate category/period observations are summed.
- Bar/line/scatter retain their existing wide and tidy-table handling. Numeric parsing accepts signed values, thousands separators, currency symbols, percentages and scientific notation. Ambiguous decimal-comma numbers are not guessed.
- Scatter X/Y grid intervals are independently editable; zero means automatic. Excessively dense intervals (over 200 lines) fall back to automatic spacing.

Map label placement measures a fixed 11px text block, considers several wraps, searches in screen coordinates and rejects positions that cross boundaries, holes, other labels or pin footprints. Labels that cannot fit at the current scale use outside callouts; zooming in may bring them inside. No algorithm can fit arbitrarily long text inside arbitrarily small shapes at a fixed readable size.
