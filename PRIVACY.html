How your data is handled

Your browser reads your table and draws the charts on your device. The website does not keep a saved copy. For charts other than maps, your table is not sent to a server. Your table and Undo history stay in this tab's temporary memory; reloading or closing it clears them. There is no account or automatic saving.

You can make ordinary charts without sending the table to us. Maps are different: finding places and showing a background map uses outside services, as explained below.

Maps use external services

Opening a map can make requests to external providers:

  • Postcodes are sent to Postcodes.io to find their coordinates.
  • Addresses and place names, with any council or country context, are sent to Esri for location matching. UK ward/council names or codes are sent to the ONS boundary service hosted by Esri.
  • Background map tiles are requested from Esri. Those requests reveal the geographic area being viewed.

Other table columns, such as amounts and notes, are not included in those lookup requests. Country boundaries are bundled with this site. We cannot promise that external providers do not log or retain requests. Avoid entering confidential addresses or other sensitive locations if you do not want to send them to those providers.

Where downloads go

Download creates a file on your device — usually in your Downloads folder, or wherever your browser asks you to save it. It is not saved to this website or sent to us. If you save it in a folder synced with OneDrive, iCloud or another cloud service, that service may also keep a copy.

HTML files contain the chart data and settings; map HTML files also contain the source table and matched locations. Someone with the file can read that data, including details that are not visible on the chart. Check it before sharing. SVG and PNG files contain the exported visual. Opening an interactive map HTML file still requests background maps from Esri.

What to be careful with

Public places and non-sensitive figures are suitable for trying the maps. Do not enter private home addresses, sensitive locations or identifying details in location fields unless you are comfortable sending them to the map providers. For confidential work, follow your organisation's rules and use a trusted device and browser. Check where downloads are saved and who can access them.

Website hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with ordinary websites, hosting and map providers receive connection information such as your IP address. The chart libraries are served with the site, without third-party script requests. There is no account, database, local-storage autosave or tracking cookie added by this application.

This describes the application's behaviour; it is not a promise about your browser, extensions, downloaded files, network logs or external providers' retention policies.
